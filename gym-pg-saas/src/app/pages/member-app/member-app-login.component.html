Tenant sign-in

Member app

To open your tenant account, use the QR code your owner put up at the property. The first time you scan it, this phone remembers the link so you can sign in again anytime.

  • Scan the PG QR with your camera
  • Tap Install if you want the app on your home screen
  • Enter your registered mobile and last 4 digits of Aadhaar

Are you the owner of a PG? Use the main site sign-in instead.
